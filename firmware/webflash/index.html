Nimly firmware

Two small boards keep a Nimly lock on Home Assistant and on the vendor app at the same time — no cloud in the local path.

1. The emulator — ESP32-C6

Acts as the lock's Connect Module towards the Nimly bridge, while the real module stays on ZHA. This image contains no credentials and can be flashed right here from Chrome, Edge or Opera on desktop (Web Serial).

  1. Plug the ESP32-C6 into USB.
  2. Flash with the button below.
  3. Add it to Home Assistant through the nimly integration (the mirror entry).

Manual flashing, wiring and OTA: see docs/flashing.md in the repository.

2. The bridge — ESP32-C3

Carries MQTT between Home Assistant and the emulator over UART, and ferries firmware updates. Its image embeds your Wi-Fi and MQTT credentials, so it is built locally instead of downloaded:

cp main/secrets.h.example main/secrets.h   # fill in Wi-Fi + MQTT
idf.py set-target esp32c3 && idf.py build
idf.py -p /dev/ttyUSB0 flash

The bridge then announces itself over MQTT and appears in Home Assistant; its Wi-Fi can also be set up with Improv over Bluetooth from the integration's discovery card.

Before you start

The mirror needs both boards: the C6 talks Zigbee to the Nimly bridge, the C3 talks to Home Assistant. Wiring, the bill of materials and the full story live in the repository's docs/ folder.
